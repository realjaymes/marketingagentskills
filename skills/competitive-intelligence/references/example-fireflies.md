# Example: Fireflies vs. Otter.ai

AI meeting notetakers, PLG-vs-PLG competitive dynamics. Figures marked _illustrative_ are placeholders. Positioning claims reflect widely-discussed public differences, not proprietary intelligence.

## 2. SWOT Synthesis

- **Strength (theirs)**: brand recognition outside sales/CS, carried over from education and journalism use.
- **Weakness (theirs)**: less depth on sales-specific workflows since the product wasn't built sales-first → maps to Key Differentiators and Why We Win.
- **Opportunity (ours)**: reposition the comparison around workflow depth, not transcription accuracy alone → maps to Customer Pain Points.
- **Threat**: if they close the CRM-integration gap, differentiation narrows to summarization quality alone → flag for next refresh.

## 3. Battlecard

**Overview**: Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting notetaker for sales, CS, and recruiting teams, integrating with Zoom, Meet, and Teams.

**Otter.ai, in one line**: a well-known AI transcription tool with roots in general note-taking, now also positioned for business meetings.

**Key differentiators** (illustrative):

| | Fireflies | Otter |
|---|---|---|
| CRM/sales workflow depth | ★★★★★ | ★★★☆☆ |
| Brand recognition | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★★ |
| Summarization quality | ★★★★☆ | ★★★★☆ |

**Why we win**: built for revenue teams with native CRM sync `[proof needed]`; action-item extraction, not just transcripts `[proof needed]`; cross-meeting search across a deal `[proof needed]`.

**Handling objections**: *"Otter is more well known."* → "Recognition and workflow fit are different things. Ask what happens to a transcript after the call — does it sync to your CRM automatically, or does someone do that by hand?"

**Questions to ask**: "Where do your meeting notes need to end up — just a doc, or your CRM?"

## 4. Win-Loss Validation (illustrative, lost deal)

**Excerpt**: "We looked at Fireflies and Otter. Otter's summaries felt cleaner to read. Fireflies had more CRM stuff but our CRM sync wasn't the main thing we were solving for yet... we picked Otter mostly because two people on the team already used it personally."

**Coded**: primary loss reason = Competitor advantage (prior familiarity/brand affinity), not a capability gap.

**Synthesis (illustrative 3-interview sample)**: 2 of 3 cite prior familiarity as decisive; 0 cite price or sales-experience issues. Confidence: Medium (needs a larger sample).

**Feeds**: the battlecard's Why We Win section should not lead with CRM-sync depth for every segment — it wins deals where CRM workflow is already the stated priority, not by default. Add a Questions to Ask item: "Has anyone on the team already used a competing tool personally?"
