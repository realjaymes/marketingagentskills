# Story Example: Fireflies.ai

AI meeting notetaker, PLG with a viral loop (the bot joins meetings where non-users are present). B2B/prosumer hybrid, Considered complexity (individual evaluates, team lead sign-off for the paid tier). Numbers marked illustrative are placeholders; do not treat them as real.

## Story Bible

### 0. Story Objective & Calibration

**Objective**: get someone with a heavy meeting load to install the bot and get value from their first auto-captured meeting.

**Calibration**: B2B/prosumer, Considered complexity.

**Output destination**: Short-Form Narrative (Mode E), for paid social and app-store creative.

### 1. The Hero

An account executive or customer success manager with 15+ client and internal meetings a week, expected to keep detailed notes and follow up on action items every time.

### 2. The World As It Is

Back-to-back Zoom calls all day. Typing notes while actively listening degrades both. Most days end with a stack of half-written notes and forgotten action items; commitments slip because nobody wrote them down clearly.

### 3. The Want

Practical: a complete, searchable record of every meeting with action items captured automatically. Identity: to be the rep who always follows up on exactly what was promised, never the one who "forgot."

### 4. The Problem

- **External** — manually taking notes while actively participating in a call is functionally impossible to do well; something always gets missed.
- **Internal** — quiet anxiety about looking unprepared when a client references something from last time that was never written down.
- **Philosophical** — nobody should have to choose between being present in a conversation and having an accurate record of it. That's a solvable trade-off, not a fact of work life.

### 5. The False Path

Typing notes live during the call, which degrades presence and still misses things. Asking a colleague to shadow-note, which doesn't scale. Trusting memory, which fails under volume.

### 6. The Guide

**Empathy**: markets directly to people who "live in back-to-back meetings," naming the exhaustion of that schedule instead of opening with features.

**Authority**: integrates with Zoom, Meet, and Teams; AI-generated summaries and action items; used across sales, CS, and recruiting teams with strong review ratings (illustrative).

### 7. The Plan

Connect your calendar, the bot joins your next meeting automatically, get a transcript and summary within minutes of the call ending. No manual setup per meeting.

### 8. The Stakes

| Cost of inaction | Reward of action |
|---|---|
| Commitments keep slipping through the cracks; trust erodes one missed follow-up at a time; note-taking keeps stealing presence from every call | Perfect recall of every meeting; faster follow-up emails; a rep who is fully present because the notes are handled |

### 9. The Call to Action

**Direct**: install and connect your calendar, free.

**Transitional**: watch a 2-minute demo of a real meeting summary.

### 10. World As It Could Be

After a call ends, a complete summary and action-item list is already in the inbox before the laptop closes. The next call with that client opens with "last time we agreed to X" pulled up in seconds, not guessed at.
